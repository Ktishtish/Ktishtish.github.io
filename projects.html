SOCWatch — AI-Powered SOC Lab

Defensive training platform that builds SOC skills through realistic, simulated phishing campaigns. Analysts practice alert triage, email/header analysis, IOC enrichment, containment, and NIST-aligned incident response — coached by an embedded AI SOC assistant.

What you practice

  • Phishing triage with severity, MITRE tags, and mailbox evidence
  • Email forensics: headers, SPF/DKIM/DMARC, spoof indicators, lookalike domains
  • Threat enrichment via simulated intel lookups
  • Containment actions (block, reset, revoke, isolate) and playbook completion
  • After-action scorecards with improvement guidance

Scenarios

  • Spear-phishing: fake payroll portal (beginner)
  • BEC: vendor invoice redirect (intermediate)
  • Malicious macro phish → ransomware precursor (advanced)
